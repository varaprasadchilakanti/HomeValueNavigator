## Dataset

`data/bengaluru_house_prices.csv` (SHA-256 recorded in `model/model.json`) is the dataset
distributed with the codebasics course
[Real Estate Price Prediction](https://codebasics.io/courses/data-science-project-real-estate-price-prediction)
and its [code repository](https://github.com/codebasics/py/tree/master/DataScience/BangloreHomePrices).
The course states that it comes from Kaggle, where it is listed as
[Bengaluru House Price Data](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/amitabhajoy/bengaluru-house-price-data)
under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication). That license is the uploader's statement; the
original source of the listings is not recorded.
